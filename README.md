# Center for Holistic Integration (CHI)

This GitHub organization implements the **Center for Holistic Integration (CHI)** meta-project philosophy. CHI defines how long-lived, interdisciplinary **meta-projects** are conceived, related, sustained, and evolved across semesters, disciplines, and technologies.

CHI is not a single project. It is an **organizational and philosophical framework** that enables projects *about other projects*, supports continuity over time, and prioritizes integration over isolation.

## How CHI Uses GitHub (High-Level)

The **CHI-CityTech GitHub Organization** is the *institutional container* for all CHI activity. Within it:

- Each **meta-project** is represented by one or more repositories.
- Relationships between projects are **not encoded via folder structure**, but via **documentation and metadata**.
- Long-term structure and relationships are defined by CHI and recorded in **CHIIDS**.
- Semester activity and student participation are coordinated through **StudentResearch**.
- Actual research and production work happens in **team repositories**.

GitHub is the *implementation substrate* for CHI’s meta-project system.

## Canonical CHI Meta-Project Model

### Institutional Layer (Implicit)

All repositories in this organization are **affiliated with CHI by default**. CHI defines:

- what constitutes a meta-project,
- how projects may depend on one another,
- how projects persist across semesters,
- and how infrastructure and coordination are treated as first-class meta-projects.

CHI itself does **not** appear as a project repository; the organization *is* the institutional layer.

---

### Conceptual Root Meta-Project

- **Balanced Blended Space (BBS)**  
  A root conceptual meta-project that provides a universal framework for describing combinative reality (conceptual, physical, virtual) and cognitive–computational symmetry.

All current CHI meta-projects are conceptually aligned with, or derived from, BBS.

---

### Primary Meta-Projects (Defined Under CHI, Conceptually Derived from BBS)

- **Blended Reality Performance System (BRPS)**  
  Systems and engineering meta-project exploring embodied, physical–virtual performance environments.

- **Collaborative AI (CAI)**  
  Meta-project defining human–AI collaboration, treating AI as a partner rather than a tool.

- **Blended Shadow Puppet (BSP)**  
  Creative and narrative meta-project serving as a major artistic testbed for BRPS and BBS concepts.

---

### Secondary / Multi-Attached Meta-Projects

These meta-projects attach to more than one parent:

- **UNESCO-Aligned World-Building**  
  Cultural research and transformation meta-project, primarily attached to BSP and conceptually aligned with BBS.

- **Bio-Aware Blended Spaces (BABS)**  
  Sensor-driven intelligent environments, primarily attached to BRPS and secondarily to CAI.

- **AI-Integrated Performance & Production**  
  Advanced production systems including Virtual Orchestra, immersive audio, and AI-driven staging.

- **Quantum / Generative Music**  
  Specialized research meta-project intersecting CAI, BRPS, and BBS.

- **AVMI / Immersive Systems**  
  Applied immersive and autonomous systems research, attached to BRPS and CAI.

---

### Infrastructure & Coordination Meta-Projects

- **CHIIDS (CHI Integrated Digital System)**  
  The central structural repository that records project definitions, relationships, proposal locations, and metadata using a relational database. CHIIDS implements CHI’s meta-project rules but does not define them.

- **StudentResearch**  
  A persistent coordination meta-project that tracks semester activity, student assignments, project cards, and links to execution repositories. StudentResearch does not store project proposals or student work.

## Canonical Rules (To Prevent Drift)

1. All projects are affiliated with CHI.
2. CHI defines the meta-project structure and philosophy.
3. BBS is a root conceptual meta-project within CHI.
4. Every project declares one **primary meta-project** and may declare multiple secondary dependencies.
5. Proposals live in CHIIDS; work lives in team repositories.
6. StudentResearch coordinates activity; it does not define projects.

---

## How to Navigate This Organization

- **New to CHI?** Start here (this README).
- **Looking for structure and relationships?** Go to **CHIIDS**.
- **Looking for active semester work?** Go to **StudentResearch**.
- **Doing research or production?** Work in your assigned **team repository**.

---

This structure is intentional. It supports longevity, interdisciplinarity, and scalable collaboration across many years of CHI activity.
